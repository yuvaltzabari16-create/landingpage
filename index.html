Features

What your staff works with every day

An S&C-grade training assistant

Set the sport, goal, season phase, level and time, and get a session in the right order: RAMP warm-up, speed and plyometrics, power, main strength and sport-specific injury prevention. Every choice comes with its rationale.

Guided sessions on the phone

Athletes see the target next to last time, log reps and load, and rate effort (RPE) at the end of each block.

Periodised plans

Phases, progressive overload and deload weeks, assigned to the whole squad at once.

New

Session sign-up

Publish sessions with a place limit and athletes book themselves. The session lands in their calendar.

Performance analytics and load monitoring

Volume, intensity, sRPE and the acute:chronic workload ratio (ACWR) for each athlete and the squad, flagged by risk zone, with athletes who stopped reporting highlighted.

New

Morning readiness check

A short questionnaire on sleep, energy, soreness, mood and stress, with a readiness score for the coach before training.

For youth academies

Built for academies, not just gyms

Parents, growth, injuries and talent: the parts of youth sport that generic training apps leave out.

New

A portal for parents

Each parent gets a personal code to follow the week, attendance, readiness, coach notes and charges, and to message the staff. Read-only, and limited to their own child.

New

Technique videos with feedback

Athletes film a set from inside the session. Coaches pause on the exact moment and leave a comment pinned to it.

New

Growth and maturation

Monthly height, sitting height and weight give each athlete's distance from peak growth, with practical load advice during the growth spurt.

New

One plan, individual numbers

Auto-adjust suggests lighter or progressed sessions for each athlete from readiness, workload, pain and last performance. The coach approves.

New

Return to play

A staged plan from injury to competition, with criteria for each stage, shared by physio and coach and visible to the athlete and parents.

New

Age-group norms and athlete card

Results ranked against your own athletes of the same age and sex, and a one-page athlete card to share with clubs and scouts by link.

Frequently asked questions

Do athletes need to download an app?

No. They open the link on their phone and add it to the home screen: on iPhone through Share → "Add to Home Screen", on Android from the browser menu.

What is the training assistant based on?

On established strength & conditioning practice: session order, loading ranges for each goal, jump-volume limits, adjustments for season phase and youth athletes, and research-backed injury-prevention exercises. It builds only from the academy's exercises and explains every choice.

How do athletes sign in?

Each athlete gets a personal sign-in code. They enter it once and the app remembers them. No passwords.

Does it work for every sport?

Yes: football, basketball, tennis, volleyball, handball, athletics, running, swimming and general fitness. Sessions and plans adapt to the sport, the goal and the level.

Can parents follow their child?

Yes. Staff create a personal code for each parent. Parents see their own child only: schedule, attendance, readiness, coach notes, growth, return-to-play progress and charges, and they can message the staff. They can't change anything.
