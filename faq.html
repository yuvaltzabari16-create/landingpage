Frequently asked questions

Answers about the platform, training, data and pricing.

Getting started

Do athletes need to download an app?

No. They open the link on their phone and add it to the home screen: on iPhone through Share → "Add to Home Screen", on Android from the browser menu. It then opens like an app.

How do staff and athletes sign in?

Every person gets a personal sign-in code. They enter it once and the app remembers them, so there are no passwords to manage.

How long does it take to get going?

An academy can be set up in a single session: create the academy and groups, add staff and athletes, then build or assign the first plan.

Which sports does it support?

Football, basketball, tennis and padel, volleyball, handball, athletics, running, swimming and general fitness. Plans and sessions adapt to the sport, goal, level and season phase.

Training and features

What is the training assistant based on?

On established strength & conditioning practice: session order (RAMP warm-up, speed and plyometrics while fresh, power, then strength), loading ranges for each goal, jump-volume limits by level, in-season and youth rules, and research-backed injury-prevention exercises. It builds only from the academy's exercise library and explains its choices. Coaches stay in control of every detail.

How is training load monitored?

Athletes rate the effort of each session and block (RPE). The platform calculates session load (sRPE), weekly load and the acute:chronic workload ratio (ACWR) for each athlete, and flags athletes outside the recommended range.

What does the morning readiness check measure?

Sleep, energy, muscle soreness, mood and stress. The answers produce a readiness score for the coach, and low readiness alerts the coach before the session.

Can athletes book sessions themselves?

Yes. Staff publish sessions with a place limit, once or weekly. Athletes book or cancel from their phone, and the booking goes into their calendar with reminders.

Is there an app for coaches?

Coaches use the same link on a computer, tablet or phone. The layout adapts to each screen.

Can parents follow their child?

Yes. Staff create a personal code for each parent. A parent sees only their own child: schedule, attendance, readiness, coach notes, growth, return-to-play progress and charges, and can message the staff. Parents can't change anything.

How does video feedback work?

Athletes film a set (about 30 seconds) from their phone, including from inside the guided session. The coach pauses on the moment that matters and writes a comment pinned to it; the athlete gets a notification.

What does growth tracking measure?

Monthly height, sitting height and weight. From these the platform estimates each athlete's distance from peak height velocity (the Mirwald method) and their growth rate, and flags the growth spurt, when jump and sprint load should be managed more carefully.

Who can open an athlete card?

Anyone with the link, so the academy chooses what goes on it (for example first name and initial only). The link can be turned off at any time.

Data, privacy and security

Who can see an athlete's information?

An athlete sees only their own records. The academy's coaches and physiotherapists see the records of the academy's athletes, and only the owner sees payments. These limits are enforced by the database itself.

Where is the data stored?

In an encrypted database with our hosting provider (Supabase), in the EU (Frankfurt), sent only over encrypted connections and kept apart from every other academy. Backups are taken automatically every day.

Can athletes under 18 use it?

Yes, with a parent's or guardian's agreement, which the academy obtains. A parent or guardian can ask the academy to see, correct or delete a minor's information.

What happens to our data if we stop?

Nothing is deleted when a subscription ends. The academy owner can export the data at any time.

Pricing and support

How much does it cost?

A monthly subscription per academy, with no limit on athletes or staff and every feature included. Book a demo and we will send a quote for your academy.

Do you help with onboarding?

Yes. We help set up the academy, the groups and the first plans, and we answer questions by email.

How do we contact you?

Through the demo form, or by email at our address.
